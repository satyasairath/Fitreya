# Fitreya help & FAQ

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Fitreya is a free personal app for tracking food, water, supplements and workouts on iPhone and Android. Your health data stays on your phone. Questions not answered here: [fit.atreya@gmail.com](mailto:fit.atreya@gmail.com).

## Getting started

**How do I install Fitreya?**
Fitreya is in testing. On iPhone it's installed through TestFlight; on Android through Google Play testing. Email [fit.atreya@gmail.com](mailto:fit.atreya@gmail.com) with the kind of phone you use and you'll get the link and short instructions.

**Which phones does it work on?**
iPhone, and Android 12 or later.

**What should I set up first?**
Fill in your body details and goal in Settings. Fitreya uses them for your daily calorie and protein targets. Then allow access to Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) so steps, active energy, sleep and workouts can come in.

## Connections

**Apple Health and Health Connect**
Fitreya reads steps, active energy, workouts, sleep, weight, resting heart rate, VO₂ max and similar data you allow. If something is missing, check the permissions: on iPhone in **Settings › Apps › Health › Data Access & Devices › Fitreya**; on Android in the **Health Connect** app under app permissions.

**Garmin**
Garmin data reaches Fitreya through Apple Health or Health Connect (turn on sharing in the Garmin Connect app), or through Strava if your Garmin uploads there. Garmin's own app decides what it shares and when, so some values can arrive later than on your watch.

**Strava**
Connect Strava in Fitreya's Settings to import your activities. You can disconnect at any time from the same place. Strava limits how many people a small app like Fitreya can connect; if connecting fails with a capacity message, everything else still works and Apple Health, Health Connect and Garmin still import.

**The same workout appears in two sources. Is it counted twice?**
No. When recordings overlap, Fitreya uses the more complete one for time, distance and calories together and says which source it used.

## Calories and targets

**How is my calorie target worked out?**
Your target is a base amount from your profile and goal, plus the active energy you burn that day. Tap the information button on Today's calorie card to see the sum. Workout calories are already part of active energy, so they aren't added a second time.

**Why does my target change during the day?**
It rises as your active energy comes in from your phone, watch or imported workouts.

**What does "Burned so far" mean?**
An estimate of the energy you've used since midnight, including resting energy. It keeps rising through the day, so it's shown separately from your target.

**Is there a minimum target?**
With a "lose weight" goal Fitreya doesn't go below a safe minimum. You can set your own target in Settings.

**Values marked "~" or "estimate"**
These are calculated by Fitreya, not measured. Values from Apple Health, Health Connect, Garmin or Strava show their source.

## Food and water

**How do I log food?**
Use the Food tab or the add button on Today. You can search foods (including Indian dishes from Indian food tables), use your favourites and recent meals, describe a meal in words, or take a photo of your plate. Always check the portions before saving.

**Photo meals**
Fitreya suggests dishes and portions from the photo; you confirm or correct them. Food photos are read and discarded: they're used only to make the suggestion and aren't kept.

**A food I eat is missing.**
Add it as your own food, or email us its name so it can be added for everyone.

**Water**
Use the water button on Today. The hydration total includes water from drinks and an estimate of water in food.

## Workouts

**Recording**
Fitreya records runs, walks and rides with your phone's GPS. Workouts from your watch or other apps are imported automatically.

**Why do time figures differ between apps?**
Some apps show total (elapsed) time and others moving time, which leaves out pauses. Fitreya labels which one it shows.

**My step count looks too low.**
Steps come from Apple Health or Health Connect. If your watch's steps don't appear, check that your watch app shares steps with Apple Health or Health Connect.

## Supplements

Add the supplements you take, with their dose and schedule (daily, weekly, monthly or when needed). Today shows the ones due that day; tick them when taken. Nutrients from supplements are shown separately from food.

Fitreya records what you take; it doesn't advise on doses.

## Sharing

The Share button on Today makes a card or story image of your day or a workout. You choose what's on it. Weight, health readings and route maps are off unless you turn them on — a route map can show where you started, such as home. Nothing is shared unless you send it.

## Feedback and problems

**How do I report a problem or suggest something?**
On iPhone, take a screenshot in the app and choose **Share Beta Feedback** in TestFlight, or email [fit.atreya@gmail.com](mailto:fit.atreya@gmail.com). On Android, email us. Say which phone you use and, if possible, attach a screenshot. Please don't include medical documents or other personal health records.

## Privacy

Your health, food and workout data stays on your phone. There are no Fitreya accounts and no adverts. Only feedback you send and crash reports come to us. Full details: [Privacy policy](privacy).

## Important

Fitreya is for personal tracking and general information. It isn't medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional before changing your diet, exercise or supplements, especially if you have a health condition.
