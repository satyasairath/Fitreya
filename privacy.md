# Privacy Policy — Fitreya

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

Fitreya is a personal fitness and nutrition app. This policy explains, in
plain terms, what the app does with your information. The short version: your
data stays on your device, and the app does not collect it.

## Your data stays on your device

All the information you enter or that the app records — your food log, meals and
macros, workouts, runs, walks, cycling, strength sessions, water intake, weight,
blood pressure, lab results, training plans and personal bests — is stored only
on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to any server, and the developer never
receives it or has any way to see it.

## No accounts, no collection, no tracking

- The app has no login or account. You do not give an email, name or password to
  use it.
- The app does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not
  contain advertising or any third-party tracking.
- Nothing you log is sold, shared or sent anywhere.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Fitreya reads information from Apple Health — such as
workouts, heart rate, steps and weight — only to show you your own fitness
information inside the app. This data is used on your device and is never
transmitted. You can grant or withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS
Settings app, under Health. Any Apple Health data is used solely for your own
health and fitness and for no other purpose.

## Internet connections

The app connects to the internet only for two optional features you choose to
use:

- **Barcode food lookup**, which queries the public Open Food Facts database for
  a scanned product. Only the barcode is sent; none of your personal or health
  data is included.
- **Importing activities** from services you connect yourself, such as Strava,
  using your own authorisation.

No other data leaves your device.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports, they are handled by Apple and provided to
the developer in anonymised form to help fix problems. They do not contain your
health, food or fitness data, and they do not identify you. Sharing crash
reports is optional and controlled by your device settings.

## Feedback

If you send feedback from within the app, it opens a blank email addressed to
the developer, which you write and send yourself. Nothing is attached
automatically — no logs, no personal data and no health information. Only what
you choose to type is sent.

## Children

The app is intended for personal use by adults and is not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a
new date.
